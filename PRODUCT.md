# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static site with the same engine as maltezskyrad.cz, lurdy.eu and velkoprevorskypalac.eu: HTML/CSS generated by `build.py` (Python 3 standard library only), content kept as files in this repository, no framework, no CMS, no client-side framework. Hosted on Vercel; the only server code is `api/news.js` (staff news publishing, committing to this repository).

## Users

Primary: applicants for the Diplomovaná všeobecná sestra programme (school leavers, and working adults for the combined form) and their families; current students (daily and combined forms) looking for timetables, plans, forms and fees. Secondary: teachers and staff, partner hospitals, the founder (Czech Grand Priory of the Order of Malta), the school inspectorate, the public.

## Product Purpose

The website of Vyšší odborná škola zdravotnická Suverénního řádu maltézských rytířů (Žerotínova 1143/38, Praha 3), a nursing college founded by the Czech Grand Priory. It must let an applicant understand the programme, the admission process, fees and open days within a minute; give students reliable documents (plans, timetables, forms); publish school news; and show the school as a work of the Order of Malta.

## Positioning

A small, personal Catholic nursing college with long-standing clinical placements in leading Prague hospitals, founded by the Order of Malta. Statements of fact come only from the existing school site.

## Operating Context

- Content today lives in WordPress at vos.mojezdravka.cz (Kingster theme): 21 pages, 4 posts (Aug–Sep 2026), 12 school-year events, 88 documents in the media library. Editorial rhythm: admission rounds and results, semester plans and timetables, school-council minutes, annual reports.
- The school uses Bakaláři (skolajecna.bakalari.cz) for student communication and Teams for teachers.
- Governing references: Sovereign Order of Malta Worldwide Communications Policy and Guide (Feb 2025); Guidelines for correct use of the two emblems and of the flags of the Order of Malta (v3.3, 2023), as analysed in maltezskyrad `docs/brand-compliance.md`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Czech only (the old site is Czech only).
- The Order is named in full where it is introduced, then "Maltézský řád"; never "Malta" alone or an acronym. The school's own legal name ("…Suverénního řádu maltézských rytířů") is a proper name and is kept as registered.
- The red shield (activities emblem) is used only as the unaltered official vector; the school lockup that combines it with the school's name needs approval by the Grand Priory / Grand Magistry.
- No invented facts. Imported pages are marked "existing" until the school reviews them.

## Brand Commitments

Grand Magistry palette (#e31a18, #f3f3f3, #c7c7c7, #454545), Asap, EB Garamond for headings (as maltezskyrad; pending the same confirmation), basic image ratio 800×470.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing school texts, staff list, contacts, documents and photographs from vos.mojezdravka.cz (October 2026). Theme stock photographs (shutterstock/unsplash, 2018–2019) and third-party press photos are not reused.

## Product Principles

1. Applicants first: programme, admission, fees and open days are one click from the home page.
2. Documents are findable: every download is labelled with what it is, its type and size.
3. One family: the site is visibly related to maltezskyrad.cz, warmer and more open, never a different brand.
4. Maintainable by school staff: news from a password-protected form, everything else in plain files.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard and screen-reader support, visible focus, 44px targets, reduced motion, descriptive link text for downloads.
